A coverage page, as an example

Home insurance for Paul Murphy is not one request. The outline is cheap. Then the expensive model writes every section of the article as its own request, and thinks before it writes. Then it writes each short heading (“Who we are”, “Our team”) as another expensive request, with the entire writing brief pasted in again. After the words are saved, each picture slot is a separate 5-cent photo. Paul Murphy was 79 coverage pages and 176 photos. PTM Specialty was 51 coverage pages and 129 photos. In scope:

  • Record the stages dropped or absent today: frame section, Problems section, answer summary, FAQ answer, SEO description, themed image prompt, blog image prompt.
  • Put those calls on the draft model log, next to planner, filler, and compliance.
  • Price grok-4.5 at $2 / $6 per million in the price table. The tick charge is the number the page shows when we have it.
  • One product-page generation on staging writes one row per real call, with tokens, model, stage, and USD ticks.
  • Those rows show on the client’s AI cost page, tied to the page. A month on that page is the cost of writing and regenerating that client’s pages. The bake adds nothing.

In scope:

The honest caveat

We know where the $168 went. We do not yet know how many dollars a full coverage page saves, because the sample was one short section with a 630-token prompt. The production brief is several times longer, and most of the 56,399 requests were never written down. The first order exists so the second and third can be judged on a real page.

Open questions

  • None that block approval. The open measurement is the dollar change on one full staging page after orders 1 and 3.

Why was the dashboard so far off?

It recorded a saved photo at 5 cents, and it never recorded a live writing call. The writing model’s private thinking is a separate charge, and the client does not store it. That is the $45. The long brief, sent over and over, is the $58.

Does the bake spend this money?

No. The bake copies words and picture files that were already saved. The spend happens while the page is being created or regenerated.

Will Entire Page still rewrite the whole page?

Yes. New article and new photos. Body still rewrites words and keeps photos. Images still replaces only the slots you select. The scheduled light edit is a different path.

Why not switch the article to the cheaper grok-4.3?

That model was only $12.65 of this bill. The article is the $120. A short sample of less thinking on grok-4.5 still read like the agency page. Moving the whole article is the larger voice change, and it waits unless a full staging page reads wrong.

Can thinking be turned off?

Not on grok-4.5. The API rejects “none.” The lowest setting is “minimal.” On the landscaper sample that cut thinking tokens from 197 to 34.

Where will we see the cost of a build?

On that client’s AI cost page in the dashboard, at the moment the page is written or regenerated. Each writing step and each photo is a line on that page, tied to the page. The month total is what it cost to create and regenerate that client’s pages. The bake adds no line.

What does done look like?

The four orders are in Factory only after approval, then shipped. A staging coverage page shows its real dollars on the AI cost page. Thinking on that run is at the low setting. An unchanged picture is not requested again. Entire Page still rewrites the page. No client site is published by this work.
